Slowgrain Cleaner for Google Drive™

Find duplicate files.

Compare duplicate and similar files in Google Drive, review them side by side, and choose what to keep.

How it works↗Contact support

Public installation and paid checkout are not available during pre-release. Contact support for availability.

01

Find the copies

Checksums identify byte-identical files. Image fingerprints help surface changed resolution, compression, rotation, and crop candidates.

02

Compare before cleanup

Review files as a detailed list, information tiles, or a thumbnail gallery. Use comparison views and overlap timecodes for supported media.

03

Keep control

Choose preservation rules and review selections before moving files to Trash. Inspect cleanup history and request restoration.

PRIVACY

Your files stay in your workflow.

Indexes and history are stored in your own hidden Drive app data. Media is processed in your browser or by an optional helper on your computer. Apps Script handles requested Google API operations and preview requests.

Privacy ↗
AUTOMATION

Useful even when your computer is off.

User-enabled Apps Script schedules can check indexed files and run supported exact-duplicate and image cleanup. Detailed FFmpeg video analysis needs the local helper and a running computer.

Similarity scores describe candidates, not the probability of an identical file. The app does not empty Trash. Trashed files follow Google’s retention and storage rules.
