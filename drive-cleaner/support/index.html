Frequently asked questions

Does the app scan all of Drive?

On Pro, the user can build an index for all of Drive or a selected folder scope. There is also a separate flow for files selected in Picker. The app requests broad Drive access to support these features.

Where does the app store file contents?

Indexes, derived fingerprints, reports, and cleanup history are stored in the user's hidden Drive app data folder. Images and short videos may be processed in the browser. Larger videos, additional codecs, documents, and audio require the separately installed local helper; temporary source files are processed on the user's computer.

Is a similarity score the probability that files are identical?

No. It is a candidate score from the comparison algorithm. Review previews and metadata, and compare important files yourself.

Does cleanup permanently delete a file?

No. Files the user selects are moved to Drive Trash. The app does not empty Trash. Users restore or permanently delete files in Drive.

Can scheduled cleanup move files automatically?

Only when a Pro user separately enables it for a selected scope and rule. Whole-Drive and folder automation is limited to exact duplicates and image candidates that pass safety checks. Crop, partial-video, document, and audio candidates are not automatically cleaned up. Scheduled runs can be stopped in the app or by deleting app data.

How do I delete app data?

In Settings & licence, open Privacy and app data → Delete my app data. This deletes the app's indexes, fingerprints, reports, history, settings, license, triggers, and current-browser cache without changing original Drive files. The local-helper cache is separate; stop the helper and manually remove .cache/drive-cleaner-helper in your home folder (on Windows, %USERPROFILE%\.cache\drive-cleaner-helper).

When contacting support

Include the error text, browser/OS, feature used, and approximate file count. Do not send file names, sharing links, OAuth tokens, or full payment-card numbers.

How do I restore or stop automated cleanup?

Open Activity history, inspect the operation and choose its restore action. You can also restore files in Drive Trash while Google still retains them and your permissions allow it. Stop schedules in Scheduled cleanup. Cloud schedules do not run detailed FFmpeg analysis when your computer is off. Moving files to Trash alone may not reduce storage use.

How do I revoke access?

Delete app data first if you want to remove stored indexes and settings. Then visit Google Account connections and remove the app. Revoking authorization alone does not delete app data.
