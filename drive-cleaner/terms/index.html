2. Service and authorization

The app works only on files you pick in Google Picker or send from Google Drive's "Open with" menu. It compares their metadata and supported content fingerprints, shows exact duplicate and similar photo or video candidates, and keeps a cleanup history. It does not scan your whole Drive or find new files on its own. The separately installed local helper is optional and requires your explicit connection; it processes requested originals on your computer.

You must have permission to process the files and comply with your organization's rules. You choose the files, selections and schedules. Similarity scores are candidate scores, not proof of identical content. Review important files and keep backups before cleanup.

The app moves files to the Drive bin and can request restoration. It does not empty the bin or permanently delete files. Google's retention and storage rules apply to files in the bin; moving a file to the bin does not free storage until the bin is emptied. Restoration can fail if the file was permanently deleted, access changed, or Google is unavailable.

3. Automation and limitations

Scheduled actions run only after you turn them on, and they recheck only the files already in your list. You can stop schedules in the app or delete app data. Scheduled cleanup of exact duplicates and supported images follows the selected rules and safety checks. Crop, partial-video, document and audio candidates are not cleaned up automatically.

Browser media analysis depends on file size, format, codec and available memory. Detailed analysis of large videos requires the local helper on a running computer. No service can guarantee detection of every duplicate, no false positives, or availability in every environment.
