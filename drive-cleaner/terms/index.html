2. Service and authorization

The app compares file metadata and supported content fingerprints within a Drive scope you choose. It presents duplicate and similarity candidates, file-list tools, storage reports, and cleanup history. Whole-Drive features require broad Drive permission. The separately installed local helper is optional and requires your explicit connection; it processes requested originals on your computer.

You must have permission to process the files and comply with your organization’s rules. You choose scan scopes, selections, and schedules. Similarity scores are candidate scores, not proof of identical content. Review important files and maintain backups before cleanup.

The app moves files to Drive Trash and can request restoration. It does not empty Trash or permanently delete files. Google’s retention and storage rules apply to trashed files; moving a file to Trash does not necessarily release storage immediately. Restoration can fail if the file was permanently deleted, access changed, or Google is unavailable.

3. Automation and limitations

Scheduled actions run only after you enable them. You can stop schedules in the app or remove app data. Scheduled exact-duplicate and supported image cleanup follows the selected rules and safety checks. Crop, partial-video, document, and audio candidates are not automatically cleaned up.

Browser media analysis depends on file size, format, codec, and available memory. Detailed analysis of large videos and additional formats requires the local helper and a running computer. Cloud schedules do not start FFmpeg on a switched-off computer. No service can guarantee detection of every duplicate, no false positives, or availability in every environment.
