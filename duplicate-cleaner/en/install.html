Install guide

Download

  • Choose Download the free scan edition on the product page. The files come from the MORAON Studio download server (download.moraonstudio.com).
  • There is one installer. Without a key it is the free scan edition; with a key it opens the features you bought.
SystemFile
Windows 10/11 (64-bit)Slowgrain-Duplicate-Cleaner-Setup-(version)-win-x64.exe
macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later)Slowgrain-Duplicate-Cleaner-(version)-mac-arm64.dmg
macOS 13 or later, Intel MacSlowgrain-Duplicate-Cleaner-(version)-mac-x64.dmg

To check which Mac you have, open the Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip" means Apple silicon, "Processor" means Intel.

Enter your licence key

  1. The first time you open the app, the Licence key window appears.
  2. Paste the key you received (it starts with SG3-) and choose Activate. The features you bought open.
  3. Without a key, choose Start free. You can enter a key later with Enter key at the bottom left of the app.

The key is checked offline on your computer. There is no account or sign-in. One key works on up to two of your own computers.

Components downloaded on first use

To keep the installer small, two parts are downloaded once, the first time you use them. Download or remove them in the app under Scheduled scans → Optional components.

  • Sharper document previews (LibreOffice, about 285–360 MB): shows the first page of PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files as a picture. Without it you see the system preview or some of the text.
  • Video style model (about 146 MB): the app asks the first time you run style sorting.

They come from their publishers (The Document Foundation, Hugging Face) and are checked against the originals by size and SHA-256. None of your files is sent.

Windows 10/11

The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows warns you the first time. Allow it once and it opens normally afterwards.

  1. Run the downloaded …-win-x64.exe.
  2. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" window appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Choose where to install and install. A desktop shortcut is created.

Antivirus software may be wary of unsigned programs. Check that the file's SHA-256 matches SHA256SUMS.txt on the download server (download.moraonstudio.com/duplicate-cleaner/(version)/SHA256SUMS.txt), then allow the file.

macOS 13 or later

The Mac app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

  1. Open the dmg and drag the app into Applications.
  2. Open the app. If macOS asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet, choose Open. It asks only the first time.

If macOS says it cannot verify the developer, or that Apple cannot check the app for malicious software, you have an older unsigned file. Download the latest dmg again.

If macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", the download is broken. Download the dmg again; if it happens again, contact us.

Updating

  • When a new version is out, the app's settings show a notice that opens the product page.
  • Windows: run the new installer. It shows the installed and the new version and says it will update the installed app; choose OK. If the app is open or in the tray, it saves its work and closes before the files are replaced. There is no need to uninstall first.
  • Mac: open the new dmg and double-click the app inside. It checks the version in Applications and offers to Update: the installed app is closed and moved to the Bin, the new one is put in its place and opened. Dragging the app into Applications and choosing Replace also works (quit the app first).
  • The first start after an update shows "Updated: old → new version" once at the top of the window.
  • Settings, the licence key, the cleanup history and scan caches live outside the app (Windows %APPDATA%, Mac ~/Library/Application Support) and stay after updating.

Cleaning up Google Drive

  • Google Drive for desktop (drive.google.com/download) must be installed and signed in on the computer.
  • The app never asks for your Google account. It reads the folders Drive for desktop provides.
  • Streaming mode is fine: exact duplicates and similar photos and videos are found without downloading the originals.
