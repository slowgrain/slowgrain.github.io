1. What the app processes on your computer

The app keeps the following in its data folder on your computer only:

  • settings and the folders you added
  • checksums and image or video fingerprints used to compare files
  • the cleanup history: paths, sizes and checksums of files it moved
  • small preview images of documents, at most 300 MB
  • photo and video details read for custom foldering: capture date, location, device and keywords
    • Locations are turned into city names using a city list built into the app.
    • They are never sent to a map service.
  • your licence key
    • It contains an order or serial number, the features and the issue date.
    • It contains no name or email address.
  • if you turn on alerts: your mail app password and KakaoTalk token, encrypted with the operating system's secure storage

Data folders:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Slowgrain Duplicate Cleaner
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Slowgrain Duplicate Cleaner
  • Mac App Store copy: the app's sandbox container

Deleting the folder, or uninstalling the app and deleting it, removes everything.

2. Network connections the app makes

The app connects to the internet only in these cases:

  • New-version check (our own download only; can be turned off)
    • Once a day it reads one public file, https://moraonstudio.com/slowgrain/duplicate-cleaner/version.json.
    • The request contains no file or computer information.
    • Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the web host (Cloudflare).
    • Store copies do not do this.
  • Optional components (our own download only, and only when you ask)
    • Turning on sharper document previews downloads LibreOffice once from The Document Foundation (download.documentfoundation.org); the first video style sorting downloads a model file once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co).
    • The download is checked against the original by size and SHA-256 and kept in the app's data folder on your computer.
    • The request contains no file or computer information; like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that publisher.
    • Store copies include both and download neither.
  • Alerts you turn on
    • Email: sent from your own mail account through your provider's mail server, to the address you choose.
    • KakaoTalk: sent to your own "chat with myself" through Kakao's API, after you sign in to Kakao and agree.
    • Alerts contain counts and sizes only, never file names, paths or images.
  • Google Drive for desktop
    • The app reads the folders and the local index that Google Drive for desktop keeps on your computer.
    • It does not sign in to Google or use the Google Drive API.
    • When thumbnails are shown, the operating system or Google Drive for desktop may fetch them from Google.
    • Some optional video checks may make Google Drive for desktop download parts of streamed files; the app tells you before it does.
  • Purchases inside the app (Mac App Store copy)
    • Purchases and restores are handled by Apple.
    • We receive no payment details.
